Your wedding day moves fast. Between the emotions, the guests, and the timeline, the last thing you want is to feel like a photoshoot is hijacking your celebration. Here are five tips I share with every couple before their big day.

## 1. Trust the Process

The best wedding photos happen when you stop performing for the camera. Laugh with your partner, hug your parents, dance badly — I'll be there to capture it all.

## 2. Schedule Golden Hour

If your timeline allows, carve out 20–30 minutes during the last hour of daylight for couple portraits. The light is magical, and it's a quiet moment together amid the chaos.

## 3. Don't Skip the First Look

A private first look creates some of the most emotional images of the day. It's also a chance to calm nerves before the ceremony.

## 4. Give Yourselves Space

Build buffer time into your schedule. Rushed photos feel rushed. When there's room to breathe, the images reflect that ease.

## 5. Hire Someone Whose Style You Love

This sounds obvious, but it's the most important tip. If you love candid, documentary-style work, hire someone who specializes in that — not someone whose portfolio is full of heavily posed shots.
