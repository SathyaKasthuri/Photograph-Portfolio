Every photograph begins long before I press the shutter. It starts with a conversation — understanding who you are, what this moment means to you, and what you hope to remember years from now.

## The Consultation

Before any session, I spend time getting to know my clients. For weddings, this might mean a coffee meeting where we talk about the day's timeline and the people who matter most. For portraits, it's about understanding how you want to feel when you see the final images.

## On Location

I arrive early. Always. Those quiet minutes before a session begins are when I scout light, compose mental frames, and settle into the space. I shoot with intention but without rigidity — the best moments are rarely the ones I planned.

## The Edit

Editing is where the story comes together. I aim for a timeless, warm palette that honors natural skin tones and the quality of light from the day. Every image that makes it to your gallery has earned its place.

## What Clients Can Expect

- A relaxed, guided experience (no awkward posing required)
- Sneak peek images within 48 hours
- Full gallery delivered within 3–4 weeks
- High-resolution files for print and web
